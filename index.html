SCDS probe tools

Current

Threshold staircase

Measures the level at which full compensation fails, separately per ear, and reports it in dB with a confidence interval. Most trials sit near threshold rather than at maximum, so it is gentler and more sensitive than the fixed-level design. ~8 minutes.

 Previous

Fixed-level 2×2 factorial

Steady vs. drifting tone crossed with sea vs. clean ambient, all at maximum level. Kept for replication of the earlier sessions. ~21 minutes, and 94% of its trials come back symptomatic.
