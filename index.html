SCDS probe tools

Current

DVA — legibility under probe

Replaces the symptom rating with a mostly-involuntary readout: a random number flashes for ~0.5 s in the middle of a 1 s probe, scored right/wrong against what you actually type, not just whether you say you could read it. Fixed-list mode reports legibility by repeat number, so habituation to a specific repeated sound shows up as a trend instead of an argument. Random-sweep mode draws a fresh frequency (plus random drift/side) every trial — nothing to repeat, nothing to learn to predict. Aggressive-random mode goes further still: a fresh random-width frequency band plus a randomly chosen sampling function (sweep, triangle, quadratic, random jumps, or several simultaneous tones) every trial, reported as a frequency × band-width legibility heat-map. Editable frequency list, adaptive sham monitoring, pause between trials.

 Previous

2×2×2 — frequency × drift × side

20 Hz vs 500 Hz, steady vs aggressively drifting, left vs right, fully crossed, with silent sham trials and a self-paced washout. Superseded by the DVA tool above: with the same condition repeated dozens of times, a within-session habituation effect can't be told apart from a real one using a self-reported severity rating.

 Previous

Threshold staircase

Measures the level at which full compensation fails, separately per ear, in dB. Superseded: the desktop session found no dose-response across 12 dB, so there is no threshold for a staircase to converge on, which is how it in fact behaved.

 Previous

Fixed-level 2×2 factorial

Steady vs. drifting tone crossed with sea vs. clean ambient, all at maximum level. Kept for replication of the earlier sessions. ~21 minutes, and 94% of its trials come back symptomatic.
