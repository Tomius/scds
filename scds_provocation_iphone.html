SCDS Infrasound Probe — iPhone

Standalone variant for iOS Safari only. One session tests one frequency (default 20 Hz — the most provocative in testing so far), delivered as an undistorted sine, with laterality (left / right / both / anti-phase) balanced across the session.

Two factors are crossed and independently balanced, so neither can confound the other: is the tone steady or drifting (±15% frequency wander in smooth 0.5–1s glides — the primary question), and is the sea/surf sound on or off for that trial. Because ambient is balanced equally within each predictability arm, the steady-vs-drifting result stays fully powered regardless of whether ambient turns out to matter — the only thing that costs precision is the (secondary, exploratory) question of whether ambient changes the *size* of the predictability effect. Neither factor is shown on screen — no on-screen tag says which condition a given trial is, since that would just be telling you what reaction is expected.

Rate each trial with the 4-level buttons once you've had a chance to notice a symptom and attempt compensation.
⚠ Read before proceeding

This delivers low-frequency audio at full system volume that may provoke oscillopsia, imbalance, nausea, or vertigo. Do not proceed if standing, near fall hazards, driving, or in an active severe vestibular episode.

Stop immediately with the red STOP button on pain, sharp tinnitus increase, or disabling vertigo. Do not drive for at least 30 minutes afterwards.
