SCDS Infrasound Probe — iPhone

Standalone variant for iOS Safari only. Tests exactly three fixed frequencies — 10, 20, 30 Hz — each delivered at full amplitude, with laterality (left / right / both / anti-phase) chosen at random on every trial. No calibration staircases: kept deliberately simple so there's nothing subtle that could quietly go wrong and undermine the results. A recorded sea/surf sound loops continuously in the background for the whole session.
⚠ Read before proceeding

This delivers low-frequency audio at full system volume that may provoke oscillopsia, imbalance, nausea, or vertigo. Do not proceed if standing, near fall hazards, driving, or in an active severe vestibular episode.
